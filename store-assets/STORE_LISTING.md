## Full Description (paste into Chrome Web Store description field)

**Stop guessing. Start scoring.**

Most people get poor AI responses because their prompts are vague, missing context, or structurally weak. PromptIQ Pro scores your prompt in real time — before you hit send — and tells you exactly what's missing.

---

**How it works**

Type your prompt in Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini. A score badge appears instantly in the corner. Click it to see your full breakdown.

- 🟢 **85–100 (A)** — Strong prompt, ready to send
- 🟡 **55–84 (B/C)** — Good, but improvable
- 🔴 **0–54 (D/F)** — Missing key elements, likely to produce weak output

---

**Features**

✦ **Real-time scoring** — See your score update as you type. No button to click, no waiting.

✦ **8 proven frameworks** — COSTAR, RTF, RISEN, CRISPE, APE, CARE, TAG, BROKE. Auto-detects the best one for your prompt type, or choose manually.

✦ **Fix My Prompt** — One click rewrites your prompt with bracketed placeholders for every missing element. Fill in the brackets, send a stronger prompt.

✦ **Platform Adapter** — Rewrites your prompt in the optimal style for Claude (XML tags), ChatGPT (Act-as + markdown), or Gemini (natural language).

✦ **Task-type selector** — Tell it what you're working on (Content Writing, Coding, Research, Business, Quick Task) and it picks the right framework automatically.

✦ **Element checklist** — See exactly which elements are present (✓) and which are missing (✗), with evidence from your own text.

✦ **Improvement suggestions** — Ordered by impact. Fix the highest-weight missing elements first.
